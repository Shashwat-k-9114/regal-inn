The valley, at your window.

Wake to the river below and the Pir Panjal above. The light changes on the peaks all day long; the house is built to let you watch it.

The house

Kept small, on purpose.

The Regal Inn sits on the old Left Bank road to Naggar, a few minutes past the Green Tax Barrier and far enough from the Mall Road to hear the river. It is run like a family house: the same faces at the door each morning, tea brought before you ask, and a fire laid in the evening.

Every stay is looked after by Suvin Hospitality, whose people know the valley by its seasons: when the apples ripen, when the passes open, and which road is quiet today.
